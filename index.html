Gameplay, Systems & Graphics Programmer
Unreal Engine 5 · Unity · C++ · C# · SDL2

Six years in game development, four of them across real-time strategy and VR training simulation. I currently lead the battle and overworld layers of a Total War style strategy title in UE5, where soldiers are simulated on Mass Entity and rendered as instanced meshes with baked vertex animation. Most of my work is simulation at scale, AI, the rendering path that gets four thousand of them on screen, and the systems other people on the team build content with. Outside work I write engines and renderers from scratch in C++ and SDL2.
