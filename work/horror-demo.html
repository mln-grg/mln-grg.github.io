Horror Game Demo

A first person story horror demo that never leaves one house. Built in Unity over the winter of 2020, structured after What Remains of Edith Finch: the house is the level, each room is a chapter, and walking into one is how you turn the page.

Engine
Unity
Perspective
First person
Built
Dec 2020 to Jan 2021
Source
github.com/mln-grg/Horror-Prototype

One house, on purpose

A single building is a hard constraint that solves more problems than it makes. There is no streaming, no level transitions, no navigation problem, and no budget spent on places the player will walk past once. Everything that is left goes into the rooms, and every room can afford to be authored rather than assembled.

Edith Finch is the reference because it does the same thing and never has to invent a reason for you to keep going. The house is the reason. You want the next room because the last one told you something.

Lighting instead of a monster

Most of the fear budget went into what you cannot see. The screenshot above is a corridor with almost no light in it, one red source at the far end, and a shape between you and it. Nothing in that frame is chasing anybody. It is unpleasant to look at anyway, and it costs one light and one silhouette.

This is early work and it sits on the site as early work. It is here because it is the first thing I built where the design question came before the code question, and because the answer to that question shaped what got built rather than the other way round.
