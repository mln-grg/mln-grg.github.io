NPC Scheduler & Gossip Simulation

Forty two people on their own daily schedules, who trade what they know about you. A prototype in TypeScript, on a hand drawn map.

Type
Personal prototype
Built with
TypeScript, Vite, Canvas 2D, Claude Code
Population
42 NPCs
Source
github.com/mln-grg/ProceduralNarrative
  • Everything the world knows is a fact. What you do becomes a fact seeded into whoever saw it, and it spreads on its own from there.
  • Gossip has weight. Facts move between co-located NPCs, weighted by heat, freshness and how much of a gossip the teller is. Reaching the watch turns a fact into a bounty.
  • 42 NPCs on a ten minute clock, each running their own schedule while the world keeps moving.
  • Opinion and faction standing shift with bonds between NPCs, so harming one person turns their friends against you.
  • Six quest threads with genuinely different endings, on a hand drawn Canvas 2D map.
