Hold of Whiterun

A text driven fantasy sandbox with a living top-down map, in TypeScript. Forty two people with schedules and opinions, who tell each other what you did.

Type
Personal project
Built with
TypeScript, Vite, Canvas 2D
Population
42 NPCs
Source
github.com/mln-grg/ProceduralNarrative
  • Everything the world knows is a fact. What you do becomes a fact seeded into whoever saw it, and it spreads from there without further input from you.
  • Knowledge propagation. Facts move between co-located NPCs, weighted by heat, freshness and how much of a gossip the teller is. Secrets need trust to move at all. When a fact reaches the watch it becomes a bounty.
  • 42 NPCs on schedules. Ten minute clock, every action costs time, and the world runs while it passes.
  • Opinion and standing. Per person disposition plus per faction standing, with bonds between NPCs, so harming one person turns their friends.
  • Crime. Witnesses are rolled against Guile. What nobody saw did not happen, until someone repeats it.
  • Six quest threads with genuinely different endings, and a hand drawn map on Canvas 2D.
