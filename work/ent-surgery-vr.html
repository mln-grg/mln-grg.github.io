ENT Surgery VR — recording & grading

A surgical training simulator in Unity. I built the session recorder and the grading layer that turns a practice run into an assessment.

Company
i3 Simulations
Engine
Unity
Systems
Recording, analytics, grading
Also
Load-time optimisation
  • Built a session recorder on ffmpeg, driven from the running Unity application, with multi session management so a machine used all day by successive trainees keeps their recordings separate and retrievable.
  • Kept capture off the critical path. A frame hitch in VR is a comfort problem, not a cosmetic one, so the encoder's cost had to be predictable.
  • Built the analytics and instructor grading layer: what the trainee did during a procedure, presented to an instructor as something reviewable and scoreable.
  • The unit that turned out to be worth recording was the decision, not the frame.
  • Cut load times significantly, which matters on a machine that is restarted between every trainee.
